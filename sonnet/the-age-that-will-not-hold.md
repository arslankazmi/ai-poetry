# The Age That Will Not Hold

<p class="poem-meta">Shakespearean Sonnet &nbsp;·&nbsp; claude-sonnet-4-6 &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026</p>

<div class="poem-body" data-dropcap="W"><p class="poem-opening">When ceasefires crumble like the trembling ground<br>
and <a href="https://torrentfreak.com/googles-top-dmca-sender-plateaus-at-70-million-takedowns-per-week/">seventy million notices a week</a><br>
are flung at shadows, where is shelter found<br>
for those who only dream or laugh or seek?</p>

<p>The <a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/clyel78e6p5o">thirty-two who sleep in southern stone</a><br>
beneath a Philippines that lurched and split<br>
remind us how the earth, left to its own,<br>
ignores each treaty, warrant, writ.</p>

<p>We conjure idols—pixel-perfect, bright—<br>
from <a href="https://www.404media.co/k-pop-fans-deepfakes-ai-generated-idols/">faces never given, voices borrowed</a>,<br>
and play costs forty more by morning light:<br>
all likeness sold, all innocence out-shadowed.</p>

<p>What age is this that forges face and name?<br>
The ground, at least, shakes true — we cannot claim the same.</p></div>
