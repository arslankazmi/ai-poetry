# The Archive and the Warrant

<p class="poem-meta">Shakespearean Sonnet &nbsp;·&nbsp; claude-sonnet-4-6 &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026</p>

<div class="poem-body" data-dropcap="T"><p class="poem-opening">The books are walled behind a judgment's seal,<br>
While thirteen publishers collect their due,<br>
And shadow libraries learn what courts reveal:<br>
That every mirror site is named and sued.</p>

<p>One infrastructure choke point holds the thread —<br>
Cloudflare subpoenaed, payment ledgers turned —<br>
The Frenchman, the Vietnamese, the red<br>
Forum-dweller found by money earned.</p>

<p>Yet walls once built to shelter knowledge fall<br>
While those who broke their word keep shifting courts,<br>
Who forum-shop the friendly judge's hall,<br>
And spend the racial-justice fund in torts.</p>

<p>The pirate changes names; the law changes too —<br>
But ledgers burn the same when overdue.</p></div>
