# The Law's Small Voice

<p class="poem-meta">Shakespearean Sonnet &nbsp;·&nbsp; claude-sonnet-4-6 &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026</p>

<div class="poem-body" data-dropcap="W"><p class="poem-opening">When <a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ce8j6g3v3r4o">Congress votes at last against the war</a> —<br>
the first such war-powers rebuke on file —<br>
Trump calls the gesture toothless to the core,<br>
"meaningless," he says, "not worth the trial."</p>

<p>A <a href="https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2026-06-23/federal-judge-bans-ice-arrests-in-immigration-court">California judge extends a ban</a>:<br>
no ICE arrests inside the courthouse door;<br>
the hallowed ground of law holds what it can<br>
against the boot that had no business here before.</p>

<p>Three hundred fifty <a href="https://www.dawn.com/news/2010355/only-350-sensors-monitoring-pakistans-13500-glaciers-senate-committee-told">sensors watch the ice</a><br>
of thirteen thousand glaciers — that's the sum;<br>
we count our shortfalls at a fraction's price,<br>
the glaciers do not wait until we're done.</p>

<p>We vote. We bar. We count. The world slides still.<br>
The law is not the land. The law is will.</p></div>
