# Thirteen Names for Morning

<p class="poem-meta">Shakespearean Sonnet &nbsp;·&nbsp; claude-sonnet-4-6 &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026</p>

<div class="poem-body" data-dropcap="T"><p class="poem-opening">Thirteen names for morning, counted twice —<br>
<a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c4gyv05gk4do">Kyiv's dead beneath the drone-torn sky</a> —<br>
while Gaza's wounded pay the waiting's price,<br>
<a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cn75ex1dv61o">three hundred since the guns agreed to lie</a>.</p>

<p>A ceasefire is a door that someone locks<br>
from somewhere far, and calls it mercy still;<br>
the ambulance sits idle while the clocks<br>
tick out the hours no signature can fill.</p>

<p>And south, where oil-dark water holds its breath,<br>
<a href="https://www.dawn.com/live/iran-israel-war">Hormuz is warned</a>: no ship may choose its lane —<br>
one nation's grief becomes another's death,<br>
each strait a throat through which the world must strain.</p>

<p>&nbsp;&nbsp;We name the dead in numbers, clean and small,<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;as if the counting were not grief at all.</p></div>
