# The Earth Speaks Twice

<p class="poem-meta">Shakespearean Sonnet &nbsp;·&nbsp; claude-sonnet-4-6 &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026</p>

<div class="poem-body" data-dropcap="T"><p class="poem-opening">Two <a href="https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/back-to-back-powerful-earthquakes-slam-venezuela-collapsing-buildings-in-the-capital-of-caracas">earthquakes wake Caracas</a> in the night,<br>
while <a href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/senate-rejects-measure-restrict-trump-iran-war-powers-as-key-republicans-shift-votes/">senators refuse to rein in war</a>;<br>
the <a href="https://www.dawn.com/news/2010572/us-iran-deal-may-leave-israels-netanyahu-as-biggest-casualty">Iran deal</a> has cost one man his fight —<br>
he finds himself kept outside peace's door.</p>

<p>The <a href="https://torrentfreak.com/major-brand-ads-on-pirate-sites-surged-80-in-a-year-euipo-finds/">brands flood pirate pages</a>, eighty more<br>
percent than last year — the EUIPO's report;<br>
<a href="https://torrentfreak.com/major-publishers-sue-welib-a-pirate-site-built-on-annas-archive-code/">WeLib is sued</a> for building on Anna's score,<br>
and <a href="https://torrentfreak.com/top-10-most-torrented-pirated-movies/">'Michael' tops the charts</a> for pirate sport.</p>

<p>The <a href="https://www.404media.co/the-tokenpocalypse-is-here-companies-are-scrambling-to-stop-spending-so-much-on-ai/">Tokenpocalypse</a> descends at last;<br>
the AI spend must stop, or so they say.<br>
The <a href="https://www.404media.co/are-public-libraries-becoming-childrens-libraries/">libraries reshelved</a> their adult past<br>
to children's wings — the grownup books away.</p>

<p>&nbsp;&nbsp;<a href="https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/articles/clyx746enj9o">Ronaldo scores his sixth</a> World Cup, and grins.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;The earth shakes twice. Somewhere the <a href="https://www.dawn.com/news/2010572/us-iran-deal-may-leave-israels-netanyahu-as-biggest-casualty">peace begins</a>.</p></div>
