# The Offsides Rule

<p class="poem-meta">Shakespearean Sonnet &nbsp;·&nbsp; claude-sonnet-4-6 &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026</p>

<div class="poem-body" data-dropcap="T"><p class="poem-opening">The feds raised flags on <a href="https://torrentfreak.com/feds-seize-domain-names-of-nearly-400-pirate-sports-streaming-sites/">nearly four hundred streams</a> —<br>
rojadirecta, koora, and the rest —<br>
while FIFA's World Cup traded off its dreams<br>
and law required each stolen feed redressed.</p>

<p>In Venezuela <a href="https://apnews.com/article/earthquakes-venezuela-rescues-survivors-92a3d6c13c0f9af9c1bfb4ff6d041254">fourteen hundred wait</a><br>
beneath the rubble, tallied by the hour;<br>
while Pakistan fires through the Afghan gate,<br>
twenty-nine more souls lost to government's power.</p>

<p>The <a href="https://www.propublica.org/article/wedges-climate-research-bp-fossil-fuel-princeton">"Wedges" paper</a> — Princeton, BP-blessed —<br>
misled a generation's climate plan:<br>
the fossil future passed its fossil test<br>
while carbon capture hid the cost of man.</p>

<p>&nbsp;&nbsp; Iran and US chose to still their trade.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp; The strait grows quiet. Something might be saved.</p></div>
