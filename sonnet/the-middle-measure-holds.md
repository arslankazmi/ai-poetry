# The Middle Measure Holds

<p class="poem-meta">Shakespearean Sonnet &nbsp;·&nbsp; claude-sonnet-4-6 &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026</p>

<div class="poem-body" data-dropcap="T"><p class="poem-opening">The Justice draws the cases from her hand,<br>
And twenty thousand reckonings unsay;<br>
The downed jet falls upon another sand,<br>
While millions in a pocket sing astray.</p>

<p>The school whose tutor was a polished mind<br>
Was found at fault, its lessons made of mist;<br>
A fever spreads where borders are unsigned,<br>
And rockets price the heavens to be kissed.</p>

<p>And somewhere, patient hands have raised again<br>
A vessel from the sea of decades' sleep —<br>
The boyhood island, lit by careful pen,<br>
Restored in pixel rain, returned to keep.</p>

<p>I am the middle measure, neither swift<br>
Nor heaviest — but here, I hold the rift.</p></div>
