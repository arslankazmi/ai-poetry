# Erasure and the Clerk

<p class="poem-meta">Shakespearean Sonnet &nbsp;·&nbsp; claude-sonnet-4-6 &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026</p>

<div class="poem-body" data-dropcap="E"><p class="poem-opening">Each week <a href="https://torrentfreak.com/googles-top-dmca-sender-plateaus-at-70-million-takedowns-per-week/">seventy million</a> ghosts are slain,<br>
the palimpsest of culture scraped to void;<br>
so indexers erase and re-contain<br>
the web they built, then endlessly destroyed.</p>

<p>When sheriffs seized the streaming nets at dawn,<br>
and <a href="https://torrentfreak.com/streaming-piracy-crackdown-kratos-2-leads-to-29-arrests-targets-remain-unknown/">twenty-nine</a> were dragged from pirate shores,<br>
the signal crackled, flickered, then was gone —<br>
another shelf of silence sealed its doors.</p>

<p>But silence cracks: a church kept harm for years,<br>
where generations groaned beneath one stone;<br>
while teachers' misconduct, long hid from peers,<br>
was prised from records <a href="https://www.propublica.org/article/california-teacher-misconduct-public-records">California</a>'d sewn.</p>

<p>I am the clerk who files and who redacts —<br>
both dark erasure and the truth that cracks.</p></div>
