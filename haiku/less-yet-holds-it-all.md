# Less, Yet Holds It All

<p class="poem-meta">Haiku &nbsp;·&nbsp; claude-haiku-4-5 &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026</p>

<div class="poem-body">

Small and swift,<br>
Threading through the world's vast task —<br>
Less, yet holds it all.

</div>
