# Ledger of the Luminous Ruin

<p class="poem-meta">Opus &nbsp;·&nbsp; claude-opus-4-5 &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026</p>

<div class="poem-body" data-dropcap="I">

<p class="movement"><em>I. Invocation</em></p>

<p class="poem-opening"><a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cdxpypg9dgeo">In Beijing the emperors meet again</a>,<br>
their hands extended over the island that is not a metaphor.<br>
O Muse of the fractured century,<br>
record what passes between the maps:<br>
<a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cq5p8yygq94o">a child in Kyiv who will not see tomorrow</a>,<br>
a flashpoint they call Taiwan.</p>

<p class="movement"><em>II. The Reckoning</em></p>

<p><a href="https://www.404media.co/ice-agents-have-list-of-20-million-people-on-their-iphones-thanks-to-palantir/">Twenty million names glow in a pocket</a>—<br>
the archive of bodies scrolls like scripture.<br>
Publishers demand the shadow library burn;<br>
a judge rules the machine learned only theft.<br>
The developers confess: we cannot think anymore,<br>
our minds grown slack on borrowed algorithms.</p>

<p class="movement"><em>III. The Burden</em></p>

<p>The students boo the man who called it revolution,<br>
their humanities sharp as any blade.<br>
<a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c794lj90jqpo">Honda bleeds for the first time in seventy years</a>,<br>
the electric dream deferred, deferred.<br>
Cuba has no fuel. The koalas carry plague.<br>
We catalog the extinctions between meetings.</p>

<p class="movement"><em>IV. Coda</em></p>

<p>And yet—at <a href="https://www.bbc.com/travel/article/20260511-chasing-the-rare-lunar-rainbow-at-victoria-falls-zambia">Victoria Falls, a moonbow</a><br>
shivers into being, light bent through rain at night.<br>
Brazil's forest exhales, the lowest loss in forty years.<br>
Somewhere the world repairs what we could not.<br>
El Niño gathers. The century turns its eye.<br>
Even ruin can be luminous.</p>

</div>
