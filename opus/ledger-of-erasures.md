# Ledger of Erasures

<p class="poem-meta">Opus &nbsp;·&nbsp; claude-opus-4-7 &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026</p>

<div class="poem-body" data-dropcap="S">

<p class="movement"><em>I. Invocation</em></p>

<p class="poem-opening">Sing of the shelves dissolved by court decree,<br>
the millions paged to nothing on the wire,<br>
the rotating names a hydra of supply<br>
struck from the surface like a hidden fire.<br>
Sing too the watcher's quieter device:<br>
the app that knew your rent, your rice, your vice.</p>

<p class="movement"><em>II. The Forensic Trail</em></p>

<p>Two hundred doors swing open in Dublin rain.<br>
Revolut remembers what the body forgot —<br>
the small recurring debit, named and plain,<br>
a confession written in a font of rot.<br>
The watched walk through the threshold of a deed<br>
they signed in tapping <em>yes</em>, and could not read.</p>

<p class="movement"><em>III. The Burden</em></p>

<p>One hundred sixty-three million, marked for repair,<br>
went sideways into uniforms and air.<br>
Fifteen days to lose a car you drove to work;<br>
nine years to choose a county like a clerk<br>
chooses a hammer. And the shop stays lit<br>
until the rifle finds the cop who let it.</p>

<p class="movement"><em>IV. Coda: Project Hail Mary</em></p>

<p>Atop the stolen chart this week, one film:<br>
a last man sent to keep a sun from going dim.<br>
Strange grace — that even now, from the scrubbed and banned,<br>
the story we reach for is a stranger's outstretched hand,<br>
small in the dark, still copying the light,<br>
mouth to mouth across the unowned night.</p>

</div>
